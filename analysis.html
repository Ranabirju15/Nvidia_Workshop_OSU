Trajectory Preprocessing

Before analysis, trajectories must be processed to remove artifacts:

  • Centering & fitting: Translate to center of mass, then RMSD-fit to reference structure (removes translation/rotation).
  • PBC unwrapping: For diffusion/polymer analysis, unwrap coordinates across periodic boundaries.
  • Frame selection: Discard equilibration; thin trajectory (stride) for storage/analysis speed.
  • Block averaging: Split trajectory into blocks for error estimation (autocorrelation-aware).

Thermodynamic & Kinetic Properties

  • Radial Distribution Function g(r): g(r) = (V/N²) ⟨ Σi≠j δ(r−rij) ⟩. Reveals solvation shells, ion pairing, liquid structure.
  • Diffusion Coefficient D: Einstein relation D = limt→∞ ⟨|r(t)−r(0)|²⟩/(6t). Or Green–Kubo: D = (1/3) ∫ ⟨v(t)·v(0)⟩ dt. Finite-size correction essential.
  • Viscosity η: Green–Kubo from pressure tensor autocorrelation. Requires long trajectories (μs) and large systems.
  • Heat Capacity CV, CP: Energy/enthalpy fluctuations. CV = ⟨(ΔE)²⟩/(kBT²) in NVT; CP = ⟨(ΔH)²⟩/(kBT²) in NPT.
  • Dielectric Constant ε: Fluctuation formula ε = 1 + ⟨M²⟩/(3ε0V kBT) where M is total dipole moment. Requires polarizable model or effective dipole.

Free Energy Calculations

Potential of Mean Force (PMF): W(ξ) = −kBT ln P(ξ) from histogram of reaction coordinate ξ. Enhanced sampling (umbrella sampling, metadynamics) needed for barriers > 2–3 kBT.

Alchemical Free Energy (FEP/TI): Transform ligand A→B via λ-coupling. ΔG = −kBT ln ⟨exp(−βΔU)⟩λ (FEP) or ∫ ⟨∂U/∂λ⟩λ dλ (TI). Requires careful λ-scheduling, soft-core potentials, and convergence testing.

MM/PBSA, MM/GBSA: End-point methods. ΔG = ⟨EMM + Gsolv − TS⟩. Fast but approximate; entropy estimation is the major limitation.

Conformational Analysis & Clustering

  • PCA / Essential Dynamics: Diagonalize covariance matrix of positional fluctuations. First few eigenvectors (essential subspace) capture collective motions.
  • tICA (time-lagged ICA): Finds slowest dynamic modes; better for kinetics.
  • Clustering: RMSD-based (k-means, hierarchical, DBSCAN, k-medoids). Representative structures for each cluster.
  • Markov State Models (MSM): Discretize space, build transition matrix T(τ). Eigenvalues give implied timescales; eigenvectors give metastable states.

Error Analysis & Convergence

Statistical errors: Correlated time series require block averaging or bootstrap. Effective sample size Neff = N / (1 + 2 Σ ρ(k)) where ρ is autocorrelation. 

σ⟨A⟩ = σA / √Neff

Convergence tests: (1) Running averages plateau. (2) First vs. second half agree. (3) Block size independence. (4) Multiple independent replicates. (5) Autocorrelation time τ < trajectory length / 50.

Systematic errors: Force field inaccuracies, insufficient sampling, finite-size effects, PBC artifacts, protonation state errors, missing cofactors.
